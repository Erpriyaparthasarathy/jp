What Is Digital Marketing?

Digital marketing is the process of promoting products and services through online platforms such as search engines, social media, websites and email. A digital marketing course helps students understand how businesses attract customers and grow their online presence.

Students can learn important skills such as search engine optimisation, social media marketing, Google Ads, content creation and website analytics.
