About MP Academy Chennai

Learn. Practice. Build Your Career.

Who We Are

MP Academy is a professional training institute in Chennai dedicated to helping students develop practical knowledge and industry-relevant skills. We offer software training, digital marketing, full stack development and video editing courses designed for beginners and aspiring professionals. With expert trainers and hands-on projects, we help learners build confidence and prepare for their career journey.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide quality software training and professional courses that encourage practical learning, technical knowledge and skill development.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted IT training institute in Chennai by helping students gain industry-relevant skills and explore better career opportunities.

Our Approach

We focus on expert guidance, hands-on project experience and practical learning to help students understand real-world concepts and improve their professional skills.

Why Choose MP Academy?

  • Expert trainers to guide students throughout their learning journey.
  • Hands-on projects to gain practical experience.
  • Professional courses designed to develop industry-relevant skills.
  • Software training for beginners and aspiring professionals.
  • Career guidance to help students understand career opportunities.
  • Skill development through practical and structured learning.
  • Placement assistance to support students in their job search.
