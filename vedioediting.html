What Is Video Editing?

Video editing is the process of arranging video clips, adding music, transitions, effects and text to create engaging visual content. It plays an important role in social media marketing, advertising, filmmaking and digital content creation. With the right editing skills, you can turn simple video clips into professional and attractive visual stories.
